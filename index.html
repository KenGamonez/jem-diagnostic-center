Questions, answered

Good to know
before you go.

Have another question? Call or text JEM and our team can help you confirm the details.

What are JEM's operating hours?+

JEM is open Monday to Saturday, 6:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Where is JEM Diagnostic Center located?+

JEM is located at JEM Building, Medina Street, Carmen Annex, Ozamiz City, Misamis Occidental.

Do I need to fast before a laboratory test?+

Fasting requirements depend on the test or package. Lipid Profile: 10–12 hours; FBS: 8 hours; FBS + Lipid Profile: 10 hours. Always confirm your preparation instructions with JEM.

Who can request mobile X-ray services?+

JEM currently identifies its mobile X-ray services for companies, schools, and communities. Availability, location, scheduling, and other requirements should be confirmed with JEM.

How can I contact JEM?+

Call or text 0961 937 3189 or 0947 393 9884. You may also email sppdc.jemdiagnostic24@gmail.com.
